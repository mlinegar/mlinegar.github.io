treepo

A Python package for learning and auditing compression trees. It summarizes long documents leaf by leaf, merges the summaries up a tree, and audits whether the summaries preserve what a measurement needs. It implements the methods in “What Makes a Good Summary? Learning Sufficient Representations at Scale.”
