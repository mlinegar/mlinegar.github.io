Mitchell Linegar

Political Economist

I am a political economist studying information and politics, broadly defined. I am a postdoctoral researcher in the Department of Political Science and the DI2 Accelerator at Washington University in St. Louis, and I received my Ph.D. in Social Science from Caltech in 2026. My research examines how information environments — shaped by technology, media, and institutions — influence political behavior, beliefs, and democratic outcomes. I use computational methods, experiments, and large language models to study these questions.

I also build AI tools that counter misinformation and help the public find accurate information about elections. With coauthors, I use randomized experiments with large national samples of U.S. voters to show that AI interventions — both written articles and interactive chatbots — reduce belief in false election rumors and help restore confidence in elections and other democratic institutions. I am currently working with the Missouri Secretary of State's office on MOE, an AI assistant that answers voters' questions about registering and voting from official sources and Missouri election law.
